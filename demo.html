Try the whole flow

Seven steps, about five minutes

Tip: open two tabs side by side. A change on one side shows on the other at once.

  1. Student Open MSc Computer Science at Toronto, press Check eligibility and send it. Then press Track it in My journey.
  2. Counsellor On the counsellor desk, switch to Whole team if needed, open the new lead and send the WhatsApp.
  3. Student Upload the documents in My journey. The counsellor then presses Submit application.
  4. University In the Toronto admissions portal, open the application and send an offer.
  5. Student Accept the offer and request a room. The stays partner confirms it.
  6. Counsellor Refer the student to a visa consultant. The visa partner ticks the steps up to Visa approved.
  7. Counsellor Press Mark enrolled. The student’s tracker is complete.

For partner agencies

An agency’s student, start to payout

BrightPath Overseas in Patna is a fictional partner agency with its own counsellor, Vikram Singh.

  1. Agency In the partner agency page, add a student. Pick a country and programme: each one shows what the agency earns.
  2. Counsellor The student lands on Vikram’s desk. The WhatsApp goes out in the agency’s name, then the application goes to the university.
  3. University The admissions portal shows the application as coming via Abroadversity and the agency. The rest of the journey is the same as above.
  4. Agency Open Earnings: commission falls due on enrolment, referrals when the room is confirmed or the visa approved. Request a payout.

Seeded students are already at every stage, so each side has work waiting. to start again.
